Our Story

Poured with love, one piece at a time

Artistry Studio is a small handmade resin studio in Pakistan. We turn glitter, dried flowers and gold accents into keepsakes you will treasure — phone cases with your name in calligraphy, keychains for your loved ones, coasters for your home.

  • Every piece is handmade and fully customizable
  • Made to order — nothing mass-produced
  • Careful packaging for safe delivery across Pakistan
  • International orders welcome — message us on WhatsApp
Payment policy: Half or full advance payment confirms your order. We begin crafting only after the advance is received.
